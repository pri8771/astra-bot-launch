## Gate contract

Every gate receipt records gate ID, mission, task/action, account/resource, authority source/revision, permitted operation, exact payload/source/review hash where applicable, currency/maximum total and expiry, issuer, verifier, verified time, secret reference and affected resume action. Missing fields remain null and block only their dependent effect. A reader/proposal capability cannot satisfy a mutation gate.

## Proposed finite defaults for admission

Use stricter existing limits when present. Initial proposal: one active experiment per mission; at most one reasoning planning pass per day only on new usable evidence; maximum two targeted repairs; at most three transient **read** attempts with backoff honoring provider limits; network timeout at most 30 seconds unless endpoint contract justifies a different finite value. Effect retries require verified absence or provider-qualified idempotency and do not inherit read retry rules. Collector/model/tool/token/page/byte/time caps must be explicit in the admitted adapter/job. Shared unknown monetary budget stays null and paid dispatch denied. No job may convert a subscription allowance into an assumed dollar budget.
