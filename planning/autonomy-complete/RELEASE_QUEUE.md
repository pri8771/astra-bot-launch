# Phased release and integration queue

This queue is a handoff for future admitted work. No runtime task is dispatched by this package. Machine-readable per-task dependencies, gates and next actions are in RELEASE_QUEUE.json; task cards remain authoritative for acceptance. A release worker's existing admitted work is not waiting on completion of this queue.

1. **Reconcile deltas while preparing useful work.** Each mission consumes fresh canonical evidence and any returned release receipt. The rightful writer matches existing issues before creating scope; exact missing inputs are validated once. Product/service contracts, candidate content, tests, economics scenarios and source updates can be prepared independently of blocked payment, posting or sample effects.
2. **First useful releases through existing routes.** OPO discovery/service/durable conversation and concrete offer preparation; WHB one strong reviewed explainer plus repeatable editorial queue; CommerceLint useful scanner-result-offer path through its existing operator/deployment route. BidetFit checker/guidance and Guru reviewed publishing follow available engineering/editorial capacity. Lipi catalog/provider/sample decisions progress independently, with actual sample/checkout/cutover gates preserved. Do not require every social channel or R730 migration first.
3. **Common controls and six explicit integrations.** SH-01–SH-15 qualify reusable contracts/mechanics, then each mission connects native events, effects, budget/account boundaries, owner direction, questions, receipts, measurement, exceptions and restart. Existing deterministic operators are extended or fenced over with their current owner; no duplicate schedulers. Implementations that share product files follow dependency order and future exact path claims.
4. **Real repeated operation.** Each mission executes its accepted external cycle and a subsequent eligible cycle without another launch prompt, with real destination readback, exact-source independent review, owner direction/pause and restart/restore acceptance. Evaluate honest business results; elapsed observations and Lipi's physical/sample/seven-day evidence cannot be simulated into completion.
5. **Optional R730 migration.** SH-16 and per-mission migration acceptance move one qualified owner at a time with drain/fence/restore/real readback/fallback. Windows independence and useful releases precede and do not depend on this phase. i9 remains deferred.

Capacity follows the release packet: at most two heavy engineering implementations and one small editorial/review lane initially; this is a ceiling, not evidence those sessions are currently running. OPO has its dedicated separately owned session. Reuse those assignments and release blocked capacity for genuinely independent work. CL/BF shared portfolio deployments are serialized by the four-bot parent; native source tasks can be prepared in isolated paths.

Before starting a queue item: verify exact source/spec/admission and ownership; compare its dependency receipts, input IDs and gates; reserve finite limits; record current direction and next eligible action. At interruption, preserve task/attempt, uncertainty, source hashes, remaining acceptance and the next command. After acceptance, append result and next decision without erasing original estimates or evidence.
