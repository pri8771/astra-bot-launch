# Lipi Standard: current evidence through autonomous operation

This is an executable planning proposal. It does not launch a store, mutate source or providers, spend, contact anyone or write Jira. The 24 task cards in TASKS.md/TASKS.json cover14 business outcomes and all 18 autonomy requirements; requirements.json is the machine-readable map. New task IDs are internal and Jira bindings remain null pending the designated Lipi writer.

The business objective is profitable delivered orders and a good customer experience: a buyer discovers a coherent product, understands fit/material/price/shipping, buys the exact variant, receives the expected quality, and receives an accountable support response. The operator maintains catalog facts, detects problems, prepares or executes only permitted actions, learns from results and asks the owner only for genuinely missing decisions. Sales or profitability cannot be guaranteed by engineering completion.

## Product and business rules

Reuse the restrained English-first premium American apparel/accessories/home direction and existing production assets. Hindi/Devanagari and legacy glyph placeholders stay excluded unless a new owner decision reopens them. Proposed Meridian/Open Current/Threshold Study membership is not approval. Quiet Route is conditional; Field Mark remains separately blocked. Do not expand assortment merely to create work.

Every permitted basket must satisfy the owner 20% all-in contribution floor. From the retained standard:R=listed item revenue−discounts; revenue denominator=R+customer shipping. Subtract exact product/decoration/supplier shipping, platform allocation, applicable variable and fixed processing/channel fees, 10%×R reserve and actual absorbed costs. Compute cents/rational intermediates exactly, compare without intermediate rounding and round only display. Collected/remitted pass-through tax is excluded from revenue; fee bases follow actual provider rules. Preserve the dated owner no-collection posture as a record, not refreshed legal guidance. Unknown cost or fee is not zero.

Price changes have existing conditional authority when current worst-case economics and required Jira/worklog evidence pass; do not ask again for generic price permission. Discounts are owner-approved at action time. The $99 free-US-shipping threshold requires mixed-cart proof and the relevant action approval; the prohibited discount+merchant-paid-shipping stack remains closed. Reserve stays 10% until owner revisits after 100 actual orders. Samples have no standing budget. Refund/replacement autonomous authority is$0, chargebacks are owner-only, and customer replies are draft-only with owner approval/send and 1-business-day target. Held/failed Printful orders escalate within 12h; after 3 business days unshipped, prepare a proactive notice. No paid advertising is currently authorized.

## Mission-specific autonomy architecture

Preserve `services/lipi-control-plane/` as the 11-tool repository-only read/proposal service. The proposed `services/lipi-autonomy/` integration consumes shared durable state/claims, deterministic scheduling, effect broker and outboxes through pinned interfaces. It does not add generic write/HTTP/shell tools to the read-only MCP service. Future product paths in cards are exact proposed ownership reservations; product admission must bind canonical source before editing.

| Event | Decision/action | Receipt and subsequent trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Provider cost/variant change | Recompute affected baskets; propose an eligible price update or block unsafe selling configuration | Current source and provider snapshot, margin computation, price preimage/action/readback, next observation due |
| Paid Shopify order | Join Printful line mapping and monitor acceptance/shipment/delivery; escalate exceptions | Private order/provider references and redacted lifecycle transitions; 12h/3-business-day deadlines |
| Support/return request | Draft policy-bound reply/remedy, ask owner once for exact approval | Case/draft hash, owner decision, actual send/refund receipt when permitted; otherwise pending |
| Approved organic asset due | Check account, live product, content rights/claims and grant; publish once | Post ID/permalink/payload and landing attribution; declared analytics window |
| Experiment window due | Evaluate qualified use, orders/delivery/contribution/objections; select next finite hypothesis | Immutable baseline/outcome/decision and next task/due time |
| Owner direction changed | Consume revision once; reorder unstarted work, pause before effects, reconcile inflight orders | Actual seen/applied/needs-input revision, question/answer and resumed task |

The event ledger stores mission/resource IDs, source/direction/spec hashes, provider timestamps, attempt and action IDs, fencing token and next_check_at. Order-level PII and credentials remain in approved private runtime storage; the cloud planning repository receives aggregate or opaque-reference evidence only. Signed/provider-authentic events are validated; customer/supplier text cannot grant authority. Repeated or out-of-order events reconcile by provider ID/version. Unknown/stale evidence stops only dependent effects.

There is one active store effect owner. Persist intent before submission and provider ID/readback afterwards. A timeout after a sample order, charge, post or price change is uncertain; query the receiving system before retry. Do not treat expired lease as proof the old process stopped. Grant consumption must be durable across restart; the existing in-memory Set alone is insufficient. Documentation and Jira projections go through immutable outboxes and one designated writer; Lipi writer designation is an outstanding input, not assigned implicitly to the four-bot parent.

## Repeatable decisions and acceptance

First-sales motion is a hypothesis grounded in the capsule, not an invented CRM opportunity or named customer. First inspect the buying path and sample; then use one eligible organic product/use-case post and voluntary feedback to discover objections about quality, fit, shipping and price. At an actual 7-day operations window, evaluate customer use, orders, delivery, returns and contribution separately from traffic. Low qualified visits can lead to one new content angle; qualified visits without carts to a clarity/fit experiment; carts without checkout to shipping clarity; quality problems to a narrowed product or source fix. Persist the selected next task and run when its event/due condition is met without another launch prompt.

Acceptance of autonomous operation requires TWO completed real authorized observation→decision→action→receiving-system verification→evaluation→next-decision cycles. A second prepared action or waiting record is valid ongoing state but not that acceptance. Only action classes actually proven are claimed. A permitted price or original organic-post cycle does not require unrelated refund/purchase/discount grants; disabled classes remain individually blocked. Customer paid/delivered-order validation remains open until real demand produces evidence; zero orders does not mean a fulfilled business test. No purchases are made to manufacture sales.

Owner-direction tests include “focus on first customers,”“evaluate Indian clothing suppliers,”and pause/resume. Their actual revision acknowledgement must survive duplicate delivery and restart, without widening spend or overwriting past experiments. Faults before submission, after provider acceptance, before receipt persistence and during pause must preserve one effect and one owner. Windows startup/cleanup must terminate only owned process trees, work with the mobile Mac disconnected and show0 idle inference. Exact-source independent review records real reviewer identity/model/host and at most2 targeted repairs. Optional R730 transfer needs SH-16 conditional acceptance; Windows operation does not.
