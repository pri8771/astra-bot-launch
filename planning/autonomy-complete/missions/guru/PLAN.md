## Editorial and audience journey

A reader encounters a concise sourced reflection, can inspect the cited text/context, understands it is an independent editorial project, and can ask a relevant question or raise a correction. The pipeline selects a specific source/translation/rights basis, distinguishes textual fact from tradition-specific interpretation and editorial reflection, produces original wording, checks accessibility, and gets a named cultural reviewer plus independent technical/source review. It then publishes on the intended account, collects actual response/coverage and chooses the next piece.

Reuse the pause/reading/gratitude structures where useful, without falsely recasting secular placeholders as accepted Hindu teachings. Do not invent quotations, personal experiences, miracles, human identity, religious credentials or source authority. A changed source or claim invalidates affected review. Every religious factual claim has exact source/rights/context or remains withheld. Two bounded repair rounds maximum prevents repeated generator/reviewer loops. Broad historical ingestion and private Kai/Pri data are unnecessary.

## Permissions and current platform constraints

Original-post operation requires the intended account, supported route, scopes/entitlement, current exact content/cadence grant, source/cultural review and finite existing allowance. A missing paid allowance disables paid effects; it does not trigger a purchase or hidden fallback. Routine account organization authority is reused, while account-specific verification remains an input. No fake identity, followers, contact import, unsolicited messages or platform evasion.

Reply/moderation/correction/delete are distinct action classes. AI-powered automated replies require prior written explicit X approval in addition to applicable recipient intent, opt-out and current self-serve summoning rules. Default reply behavior is an owner-reviewed draft. Original-post autonomy does not depend on receiving AI-reply permission. These current constraints were checked in [X automation rules](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation) and [X manage-post documentation](https://docs.x.com/x-api/posts/manage-tweets/introduction). Account eligibility still requires real readback; the existence of an API is not a grant.

GURU-G01–05 in UNRESOLVED_INPUTS.md apply only to their named effect classes. `effect_gates` on a card is a union, not a requirement to collect every unrelated permission. An unsupported automated reply leaves original posts and draft moderation useful. Public correction/deletion needs exact authority and readback; deleted content is not assumed recoverable.
