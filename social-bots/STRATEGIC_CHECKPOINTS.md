# Social Bots strategic checkpoints

The detailed roadmap still uses granular versions. For delivery/coordination, the owner has chosen three major strategic checkpoints.

## Checkpoint 1 — V1.7

Meaning:
A reliable autonomous social operator with intelligence through community operation.

Underlying capabilities include:
- autonomous reasoning/research;
- reliable worker/recovery;
- platform choice;
- analytics;
- audience memory;
- experiments;
- content intelligence;
- community observation/decision/memory.

External posting/reply actions remain authority-gated.

## Checkpoint 2 — V2.3

Meaning:
An autonomous growth strategist that can decompose goals and create/use temporary specialist workers.

Includes:
- V2.0 evidence-driven growth engine;
- V2.1 dynamic strategy;
- V2.2 goal decomposition;
- V2.3 specialist worker lifecycle.

Specialist workers belong to Social Bots and do not depend on SwarmAI.

## Checkpoint 3 — V3.0

Meaning:
Autonomous multi-brand media organization.

Includes:
- multiple persistent brands/personas;
- portfolio-level planning;
- brand isolation;
- shared-fact boundaries;
- resource/growth allocation;
- specialist worker pools;
- organizational memory/evaluation;
- bounded human authority/legal/identity intervention.

## Today's target

Aim for V2.0 engineering-ready.

Operational promotion remains evidence-gated and cannot be fabricated around public/account restrictions.
