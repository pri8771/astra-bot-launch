# Artifact packet — SB-V13-001

Artifact: Normalized analytics brain
Milestone: V1.3
Lane: Intelligence
Story points: 4

Goal: create a truthful cross-platform analytics model without pretending unlike metrics are directly equivalent.

Required:
- raw event/metric retention;
- normalized semantic categories (reach, view, completion, save, share, reply, click, follow/conversion where available);
- platform/source/time window/account/persona/content/experiment IDs;
- missing/unavailable distinct from zero;
- metric provenance and collection timestamp;
- derived metrics record formula/version.

Acceptance:
- missing vs zero regression;
- platform-specific metrics do not silently map to false equivalence;
- one content item can be traced from raw metrics to experiment/persona;
- stale windows are marked.


Lead review constraints from `INTELLIGENCE_CODE_REVIEW.md`:
- every normalized metric declares semantic kind such as `cumulative_snapshot`, `delta`, `gauge`, or `rate`;
- aggregation MUST be semantic-aware;
- cumulative snapshots must never be naively summed;
- retain raw platform metric name/value and observation window;
- normalized/derived metrics record transformation/formula version.

Additional acceptance:
- snapshots 100 then 150 views must not aggregate to 250 views;
- deltas 100 then 50 may aggregate to 150 when semantics declare deltas;
- a cumulative snapshot can be converted to a delta only with a valid earlier comparable snapshot and explicit derivation record.
