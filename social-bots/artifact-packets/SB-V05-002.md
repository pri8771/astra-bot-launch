# Artifact packet — SB-V05-002

Artifact: Claim-to-source factual support review
Milestone: V0.5
Lane: Intelligence
Story points: 4
Current status: CHANGES_REQUIRED after LEAD-014 review.

Goal: determine whether material factual claims are actually supported by captured evidence, not merely whether a URL exists.

Required:
- identify material claims in a candidate rather than trusting the caller to enumerate only convenient claims;
- classify claims as factual / opinion / creative;
- bind each factual claim to one or more captured evidence refs;
- support status: SUPPORTED / PARTIAL / UNSUPPORTED / CONFLICTED / UNKNOWN;
- preserve source timestamp/hash;
- unsupported required claims withhold candidate;
- opinions/creative framing are distinguished from factual assertions.

Acceptance:
- URL-present-but-unsupported claim fails;
- conflicting sources yield CONFLICTED/withheld;
- supported claim passes with exact evidence ref;
- changed source hash invalidates stale support until re-reviewed;
- a candidate containing a material factual claim not pre-enumerated by the caller is still identified and gated;
- omission of a material factual claim from caller-supplied bindings cannot bypass review.

## LEAD-014 finding

Submitted `runtime/factcheck.py` is strong once `Claim` objects and stance bindings are provided, but the worker report explicitly leaves claim extraction/identification as caller input. That misses a required part of this artifact. Add a bounded, inspectable material-claim identification stage/interface and prove callers cannot bypass factual review by omitting a claim.
