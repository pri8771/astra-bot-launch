# Artifact packet — SB-V05-002

Artifact: Claim-to-source factual support review
Milestone: V0.5
Lane: Intelligence / Evidence Integrity
Story points: 4
Current status: CHANGES_REQUIRED after LEAD-017 review.

## Goal

Determine whether every material factual claim is actually supported by trustworthy captured evidence, rather than merely whether a URL exists or whether caller-selected words co-occur.

## Required

- identify material claims in candidate text rather than trusting the caller to enumerate only convenient claims;
- classify claims as factual / opinion / creative;
- bind each factual claim to one or more SB-V05-001 evidence refs;
- operational factual review may consume only evidence acceptable under the SB-V05-001 operational trust boundary; fixture/untrusted captures remain test-only;
- support status: SUPPORTED / PARTIAL / UNSUPPORTED / CONFLICTED / UNKNOWN;
- preserve source URL, retrieval timestamp, receipt id, content hash and excerpt/span;
- preserve assessor identity + version + concise rationale;
- unsupported/unknown/conflicted required claims withhold the candidate;
- opinions/creative framing are distinguished from factual assertions;
- changed evidence hash invalidates prior support until reassessment.

## Assessor trust boundary

Operational support may come only from a closed, attributable assessor route controlled by the fact-review module/configuration.

A caller must not be able to make its own assessor operational by:
- setting an attribute;
- calling a public self-registration function;
- subclassing an operational assessor;
- constructing a SupportAssessment with `operational=True`.

A manual/fixture assessor is acceptable for tests but must be structurally incapable of granting operational support.

## Support quality

`SUPPORTED` means the cited excerpt actually supports the material proposition.

Mere token/key-term co-occurrence is not sufficient for full support. A bounded deterministic assessor may be retained, but when it cannot establish the proposition/relation/negation safely it must return PARTIAL/UNKNOWN/UNSUPPORTED rather than overclaiming SUPPORTS.

Required adversarial examples should include:
- same key terms but opposite meaning/negation;
- same key terms in an unrelated relationship;
- numeric/date mismatch;
- evidence that mentions the subject but not the claimed predicate;
- conflicting evidence.

No hidden chain-of-thought is required; store only attributable status, concise rationale and evidence span.

## Acceptance

- URL-present-but-unsupported claim fails;
- conflicting sources yield CONFLICTED/withheld;
- supported claim passes with exact trusted evidence ref;
- changed source hash invalidates stale support until re-reviewed;
- a candidate containing a material factual claim not pre-enumerated by the caller is still identified and gated;
- omission of a material factual claim from caller-supplied bindings cannot bypass review;
- a caller-created assessor cannot self-register or self-assert operational authority;
- fixture/manual assessor cannot produce an operational pass;
- verified-but-untrusted/fixture SB-V05-001 captures cannot produce operational factual support;
- key-term co-occurrence with opposite/unrelated meaning does not become SUPPORTED;
- no operational assessor => UNKNOWN/WITHHELD for required facts.

### LEAD-017

The repair at `cbd781ca...` adds material-claim identification and attributable assessments, but independent review found two trust/quality blockers:
