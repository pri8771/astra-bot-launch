# Artifact packet — SB-V05-002

Artifact: Claim-to-source factual support review
Milestone: V0.5
Lane: Intelligence
Story points: 4

Goal: determine whether material factual claims are actually supported by captured evidence, not merely whether a URL exists.

Required:
- identify material claims in candidate;
- bind each claim to one or more captured evidence refs;
- support status: SUPPORTED / PARTIAL / UNSUPPORTED / CONFLICTED / UNKNOWN;
- preserve source timestamp/hash;
- unsupported required claims withhold candidate;
- opinions/creative framing are distinguished from factual assertions.

Acceptance:
- URL-present-but-unsupported claim fails;
- conflicting sources yield CONFLICTED/withheld;
- supported claim passes with exact evidence ref;
- changed source hash invalidates stale support until re-reviewed.
