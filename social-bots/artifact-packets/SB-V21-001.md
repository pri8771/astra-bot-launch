# Artifact packet — SB-V21-001

Artifact: Persistent dynamic strategy lifecycle
Milestone: V2.1
Lane: Core
Story points: 4

Goal: turn V2.0 strategy revisions into a durable strategy lifecycle with decay, supersession and rollback.

Required:
- immutable strategy versions;
- active strategy pointer;
- evidence refs;
- confidence/expiry/review date;
- bounded revision magnitude;
- rollback condition;
- supersession history;
- no hidden mutation of prior versions.

Acceptance:
- create v1 -> evidence-driven v2 -> contrary evidence rollback/supersede v3;
- historical versions remain inspectable;
- expired/stale strategies cannot silently remain authoritative;
- no evidence => no automatic strategic drift.
