# Artifact packet — SB-V30-003

Artifact: Portfolio growth/resource allocation
Milestone: V3.0
Lane: Intelligence + Core integration
Story points: 5

Goal: allocate bounded attention/worker capacity across brands based on evidence, opportunity and learning value.

Inputs:
- per-brand strategy;
- growth opportunities;
- active experiments;
- worker/resource availability;
- uncertainty;
- authority/cost constraints.

Outputs:
- ranked portfolio allocation;
- rationale/evidence refs;
- fairness/coverage constraints where configured;
- rollback/review point.

Acceptance:
- high-opportunity brand may receive more capacity without starving all other persistent brands unless policy allows;
- no monetary spend is granted;
- one brand's noisy anomaly cannot dominate portfolio allocation;
- allocations are reversible and versioned.
