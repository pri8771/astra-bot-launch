# Artifact packet — SB-V12-001

Artifact: Platform selection intelligence
Milestone: V1.2
Lane: Intelligence
Story points: 3

Goal: choose which platform(s) deserve an idea rather than blindly cross-posting.

Inputs:
- persona/platform strategy;
- content format/asset needs;
- real historical performance when available;
- platform constraints;
- experiment/learning value;
- authority/account availability.

Output:
- ranked eligible platforms with reasons and uncertainty;
- BLOCKED when account/authority route is unavailable;
- no fabricated performance data.

Acceptance:
- different content formats/personas yield different rankings;
- unavailable platform cannot be selected for execution;
- no historical metrics => explicit uncertainty, not invented priors presented as evidence.
