# Artifact packet — SB-V15-001

Artifact: Autonomous experiment lifecycle engine
Milestone: V1.5
Lane: Intelligence
Story points: 4

Goal: design, register, observe, close and learn from bounded experiments.

Required:
- hypothesis;
- baseline;
- intervention;
- primary metric;
- observation window;
- stop/safety criteria;
- status lifecycle;
- result/effect-size or honest inconclusive outcome;
- post-experiment learning refs.

Acceptance:
- cannot close before required observation unless stop criterion triggers;
- missing metrics => INCONCLUSIVE, never success;
- duplicate overlapping experiments detected;
- result updates audience/strategy only through evidence refs.
