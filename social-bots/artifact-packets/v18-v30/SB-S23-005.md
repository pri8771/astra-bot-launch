## Deterministic vs model responsibility

Deterministic default writer: assembles a draft from `AnalysisResult/1` findings using a fixed template, every claim carrying the finding's refs. Optional provider (S23-007-wrapped) may rewrite prose; any provider-introduced claim without refs is stripped and recorded in `limitations`. Platform formatting hard gates remain Intelligence's (`content_intelligence`) and run at adoption/pipeline time, not here.

## Fail-closed behavior

- Role mismatch → `RoleMismatch`.
- No findings/evidence in inputs → `COMPLETED` with an empty candidate and `limitations=["no supported claims"]`; never invents claims.
- Module has no import of `pipeline`, `decision`, `content_dir`, `experiment_engine`; test greps for `publish`, `enqueue`, `register_experiment`, `content_dir`.
- Deadline → `TIMED_OUT`.
