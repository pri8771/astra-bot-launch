# Artifact packet — SB-V20-001

Artifact: Strategy state and evidence-driven revision engine
Milestone: V2.0
Lane: Core
Story points: 5

Goal: let each bot maintain an explicit strategy and revise it from measured evidence.

Strategy includes:
- objectives;
- priority audiences/topics/formats/platforms;
- hypotheses;
- active bets/experiments;
- allocation weights;
- constraints;
- evidence refs;
- revision history.

Required:
- strategy revisions are proposed from analytics/audience/experiment evidence;
- deterministic policy bounds changes;
- every revision is attributable/reversible;
- no evidence => no fabricated optimization;
- one bad result cannot cause unbounded strategy swing.

Acceptance:
- measured evidence causes a bounded strategy change in a controlled test;
- contrary evidence can reverse/reduce a prior;
- no-data path yields no change/need-more-evidence;
- strategy histories are separate by persistent bot/persona.
