# Artifact packet — SB-V14-001

Artifact: Audience memory
Milestone: V1.4
Lane: Intelligence
Story points: 4

Goal: maintain evidence-backed audience hypotheses that evolve with measured results.

Required:
- hypothesis ID, segment/context, statement, confidence, supporting/contradicting evidence, freshness/decay, last-updated;
- separate observation from inference;
- contradictions reduce confidence or fork hypotheses;
- no sensitive-person inference/profile targeting.

Acceptance:
- repeated support increases confidence within bounds;
- contrary evidence reduces/revises confidence;
- old evidence decays;
- no metrics => no fake learning.


Isolation requirement:
- audience hypotheses MUST be scoped at least by persistent bot + persona/workspace;
- a cultural persona sharing a runtime with a general persona must not silently write into or consume the other's private audience hypothesis state;
- shared population-level facts, if any, require an explicit shared-evidence layer rather than private-memory reuse.

Acceptance:
- two personas on one runtime can hold contradictory audience hypotheses without overwriting or blending them;
- every confidence update identifies persona/workspace scope and evidence refs.
