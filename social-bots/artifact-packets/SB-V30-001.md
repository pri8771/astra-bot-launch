# Artifact packet — SB-V30-001

Artifact: Portfolio-level multi-brand planning contract
Strategic checkpoint: V3.0
Lane: Core + lead design
Story points: 5

Goal: coordinate multiple persistent brands/personas while preserving separate identities/strategies and safe shared infrastructure.

Required:
- portfolio goals;
- per-brand goals/constraints;
- brand-private strategy/memory;
- shared-fact layer with provenance;
- resource/allocation proposals;
- conflict resolution;
- no cross-brand identity contamination;
- human authority/legal/identity boundary.

Acceptance:
- shared fact can inform multiple brands without copying private audience/strategy state;
- conflicting brand priorities resolve through explicit portfolio policy;
- one brand failure cannot corrupt another;
- resource reallocation is evidence-based and reversible.
