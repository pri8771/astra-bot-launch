# Artifact packet — SB-V20-002

Artifact: Growth evaluator and allocation engine
Milestone: V2.0
Lane: Intelligence
Story points: 4

Goal: turn real analytics/experiment/audience evidence into bounded recommendations about where to spend attention and learning effort.

Inputs:
- normalized metrics;
- experiment outcomes;
- audience hypotheses;
- platform/content performance;
- uncertainty;
- operational availability/cost.

Outputs:
- ranked growth/learning opportunities;
- recommended allocation across platform/format/experiment;
- reason/evidence refs;
- uncertainty;
- no recommendation when evidence insufficient.

Acceptance:
- different evidence leads to different allocation;
- learning value can beat short-term reach when justified;
- missing data does not become zero;
- no monetary spend is authorized by this artifact.
