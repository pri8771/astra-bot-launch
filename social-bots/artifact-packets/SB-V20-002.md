# Artifact packet — SB-V20-002

Artifact: Growth evaluator and allocation engine
Milestone: V2.0
Lane: Intelligence
Story points: 4
Current status: CHANGES_REQUIRED after LEAD-014 review.

Goal: turn real analytics/experiment/audience evidence into bounded recommendations about where to spend attention and learning effort.

Inputs:
- bot + persona/workspace scope;
- normalized metrics;
- experiment outcomes;
- audience hypotheses;
- platform/content performance;
- uncertainty;
- operational availability/cost;
- evidence refs.

Outputs must align with `CROSS_LANE_INTERFACES.md` GrowthOpportunity semantics:
- stable opportunity id;
- bot/persona scope;
- opportunity type;
- target platform/format/experiment where relevant;
- expected growth value;
- expected learning value;
- confidence and uncertainty;
- required authority;
- evidence refs;
- operational availability;
- cost class;
- ranked growth/learning opportunities and bounded attention allocation;
- no recommendation when evidence insufficient.

Acceptance:
- different evidence leads to different allocation;
- learning value can beat short-term reach when justified;
- missing data does not become zero;
- unavailable destinations are blocked;
- no monetary spend is authorized by this artifact;
- two personas on one shared runtime remain distinguishable in every opportunity/allocation record;
- output can be consumed by Core's strategy-revision interface without inventing missing authority/cost/scope data.

## LEAD-014 finding

Submitted `runtime/growth_evaluator.py` has good missing-data/no-spend behavior but `OpportunityInput` lacks persistent bot/persona scope and the result does not carry the full accepted cross-lane authority/availability/cost contract. It also depends on currently changes-required V13/V14 semantics. Reconcile the interface after those upstream repairs.
