## Lead-owned multi-match selection contract

The following contract is authoritative for exact bot/persona/platform route selection:

1. Collect **all exact-scope matches** for bot + persona + platform. Do not widen scope or infer aliases/destinations.
2. Evaluate each match independently using the existing route-type + health + LEAD-056 freshness gate.
3. Define **eligible** for route selection as passing those route-level availability gates. Capability-specific flags (draft/publish/reply/analytics) remain evaluated after selection for the requested operation; they do not cause an unhealthy/stale route to win.
4. If **exactly one eligible route** remains, select that route.
5. If **zero eligible routes** remain, fail closed as unavailable. Preserve useful factual diagnostics; do not fall back to an ineligible route merely because it is first.
6. If **more than one eligible route** remains, fail closed with explicit **ambiguous multiple eligible routes**. No registry-order selection, lexical route-id preference, newest timestamp preference, account-alias preference, or other inferred destination tie-break is authorized.
7. Registry order must not change the result.
8. The selected route's existing draft/publish/reply/analytics capability and explicit authority flags still govern the downstream operation. This contract grants no new destination or effect authority.

Rationale: there is currently no product-level field declaring a preferred route among multiple simultaneously eligible exact-scope destinations. Failing closed is safer and truthful than inventing a priority rule.
