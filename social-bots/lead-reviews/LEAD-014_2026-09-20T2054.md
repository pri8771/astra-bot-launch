### SB-V03-005 — CHANGES_REQUIRED

Claude correctly reconciled the documentation to a logical-isolation model and added `runtime/isolation.py`, but the artifact contract requires proving that every persona-specific read/filter path prevents cross-persona contamination.

Current implementation supplies safe persona-filter helpers but does not enforce their use. Existing raw bot-wide readers remain available (for example bot-wide content/publish/analytics/history APIs). A future or current caller can bypass `runtime.isolation` and read another persona's non-shared records. The logical isolation boundary is therefore convention/audit based, not yet a hard contract.

Required repair:
1. define one authoritative persona-scoped repository/read interface for non-shared stores;
2. move production persona-specific reads behind it or make raw bot-wide readers explicitly internal/admin-only and prove production paths cannot use them accidentally;
3. add tests that exercise real production read paths, not only the audit helper;
4. preserve the shared runtime-state boundary where intended.

## Intelligence lane dispositions

### SB-V05-001 — ACCEPTED as an engineering artifact

### SB-V05-002 — CHANGES_REQUIRED

The support engine is strong once claims/bindings are supplied: it distinguishes SUPPORTED/PARTIAL/UNSUPPORTED/CONFLICTED/UNKNOWN, preserves source hash/time, detects changed-hash staleness, and withholds unsupported required factual claims.

But the artifact packet also requires identifying material claims in a candidate. The worker explicitly leaves claim extraction as caller-supplied/out of scope. Add a bounded claim-identification interface/implementation (adaptive or deterministic, but inspectable) and tests proving material factual claims cannot simply be omitted to bypass review.

### SB-V13-001 — CHANGES_REQUIRED

This is the largest Intelligence correctness miss. `MetricValue` has no semantic kind such as `cumulative_snapshot`, `delta`, `gauge`, or `rate`, and `aggregate_semantic()` sums every PRESENT observation by platform. Two cumulative snapshots of 100 then 150 therefore produce 250, which is incorrect and violates the lead packet.

Required repair:
- attach metric semantics to each raw/normalized observation;
- semantic-aware aggregation;
- never sum cumulative snapshots as independent events;
- explicit snapshot-to-delta derivation only from comparable prior observations with derivation/version metadata;
- regressions: snapshots 100 -> 150 must not total 250; deltas 100 + 50 may total 150.

### SB-V14-001 — CHANGES_REQUIRED

`Hypothesis` has no bot/persona/workspace identity and persists under `memory/<bot>/audience/<hypothesis>.json`. A general and cultural persona sharing one runtime can therefore store/read private audience hypotheses in the same namespace without a persona guard.

Required repair:
- persist and validate bot + persona/workspace scope;
- persona-scoped save/load/list APIs;
- regression proving two personas on one runtime may maintain contradictory hypotheses without overwrite/read blending;
- shared facts, if desired, must enter through an explicit shared-evidence layer rather than private audience memory.

### SB-V15-001 — BLOCKED on V13/V14 repair; source review positive

The experiment lifecycle correctly treats missing primary metric as INCONCLUSIVE, enforces observation windows unless safety stops, detects overlapping active experiments, and creates learning refs only for real SUCCESS/FAILURE effects. Acceptance remains dependency-blocked until V13/V14 inputs are trustworthy.

### SB-V16-001 — CHANGES_REQUIRED

The platform repair/fact-preservation work is useful, but content-intelligence history is bot-wide. `record_variant(bot, variant)` does not store persona, and `check_novelty(bot, variant)` compares against every same-platform record on that runtime. A cultural persona can therefore make a general persona appear duplicate/near-duplicate and vice versa.

Required repair: record persona/workspace and scope novelty/history reads by persona unless an explicitly designed shared-brand dedup layer is invoked.

### SB-V12-001 — BLOCKED on V13 repair; source review positive

Platform selection correctly blocks unavailable/unauthorized routes, marks missing history exploratory rather than invented, and keeps historical signals per-platform. It cannot be accepted through its dependency chain until normalized analytics semantics are repaired.

### SB-V17-001 — CHANGES_REQUIRED

The no-public-effect boundary is good, but community memory/themes are bot-wide. `community_themes(bot)` aggregates safe signals from all personas on a shared runtime and `theme_to_audience_evidence()` carries bot but not persona. That can feed one persona's community evidence into another persona's audience memory.

Required repair: persona/workspace-scoped remember/read/theme aggregation and audience evidence refs, with an explicit shared-evidence path only when intentionally generalized.
