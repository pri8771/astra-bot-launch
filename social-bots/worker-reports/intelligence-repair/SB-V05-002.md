## Changes (`runtime/factcheck.py`)

1. **Attributable assessor, not caller-asserted stance.** Support stances are
   produced by a `SupportAssessor` (identity + version), not passed in by the
   caller. `SupportAssessment` binds claim → evidence excerpt/span/hash →
   assessor identity/version → stance. `operational` is derived from an
   operational-assessor registry (`register_operational_assessor` /
   `is_operational_assessor`), keyed on the assessor's exact class — a caller
   cannot make an assessment operational by setting an attribute.
2. **Operational vs fixture.** `KeywordSupportAssessor` is a deterministic,
   inspectable operational assessor (checks the claim's key terms against the
   cited excerpt; detects refutation cues; rationale names the drivers).
   `ManualAssessor` echoes a caller-declared stance but is `operational=False`
   and never grants support — the fixture/test-only path.
3. **No operational assessor => UNKNOWN/WITHHELD.** A required factual claim
   backed only by non-operational assessments resolves to `UNKNOWN` and is
   withheld.
4. **Material-claim identification (closes LEAD-014).** `HeuristicClaimExtractor`
   is a bounded, inspectable identifier: it splits candidate text into sentences
   and flags factual assertions (numbers/years or factual phrasing) as material
   claims while marking subjective/creative sentences. `reconcile_claims` /
   `review_candidate` merge caller claims with identified ones; a material
   factual claim the caller omitted is added as a required claim with no support
   → withheld. This proves omission cannot bypass factual review.
5. Preserved staleness semantics (changed content hash invalidates support) and
   the `SUPPORTED/PARTIAL/UNSUPPORTED/CONFLICTED/UNKNOWN` vocabulary. Exposed
   `ClaimSupportResult` for SB-V16-001 consumption.

No Core files modified.

## Known limits

- `KeywordSupportAssessor` is deterministic keyword/cue matching, not semantic
  entailment; it is intentionally simple and auditable. A model-backed
  operational assessor can register through the same seam later (fail-closed:
  absent an operational assessor, required factual claims are withheld).
- `HeuristicClaimExtractor` is marker-based; it favours recall on factual
  markers (fail-closed toward gating) and can over-identify. A richer extractor
  can replace it behind the `ClaimExtractor` protocol.
