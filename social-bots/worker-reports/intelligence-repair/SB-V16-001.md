## Changes (`runtime/content_intelligence.py`)

1. **Fact bindings must resolve to an acceptable ClaimSupportResult.**
   `Segment.fact` now requires a `claim_id` and a `support_status` in
   `{SUPPORTED, PARTIAL}`; `Segment.fact_from_support(text, claim_result)` builds
   a binding from a factcheck `ClaimSupportResult` and refuses anything not
   SUPPORTED/PARTIAL. An unsupported/conflicted/unknown claim can never back
   published content.
2. **Persona-scoped private history + novelty.** `PlatformVariant` carries
   `persona`; history persists under `content/<bot>/<persona>/`. `check_novelty`
   scans the variant's OWN persona history by default, so a cultural persona's
   similar content cannot make a general persona look duplicate (and vice versa).
3. **Explicit shared-brand dedup layer.** `record_variant(..., shared_brand=True)`
   and `check_novelty(..., shared_brand=True)` use a separate, opt-in shared-brand
   namespace; it is never the default and never mixes personas' private history.
4. Preserved: platform-native constraints, hook/format/asset plan, fact
   preservation (facts verbatim, framing compressed), over-limit repair-or-withhold
   (no silent truncation), near-duplicate shingle detection, series/repurpose
   lineage, distinct per-platform variants.

No Core files modified.
