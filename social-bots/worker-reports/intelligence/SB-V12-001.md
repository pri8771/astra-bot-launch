## What was built

New `runtime/platform_selection.py` — chooses which platform(s) deserve an idea
instead of blind cross-posting.

### Model
- `select_platforms()` gates on availability/authority first: a platform whose
  account/authority route is unavailable is **BLOCKED** and never selectable.
- A platform that cannot carry the required native format (per `PLATFORM_CAPS`)
  is **UNSUITABLE** (also not selectable).
- Eligible platforms are scored from: persona/platform strategy weight,
  real per-platform historical performance *when available*, and learning value.
- **No fabricated performance:** with no history, `historical_basis` is `None`,
  uncertainty is HIGH, and reasons state the ranking is EXPLORATORY — no invented
  prior is presented as evidence. Historical signals are treated as per-platform
  indicators (no cross-platform false equivalence, per SB-V13-001).
- Learning value can outrank a modest known performer (explore for information
  gain) — configurable via `learning_weight`.
- Output: ranked platforms with reasons + uncertainty, and explicit
  `selectable` / `blocked` / `unsuitable` lists.

## Known limits (explicit, not faked)
- Availability/authority and per-platform historical performance are supplied by
  the caller (from the account map and SB-V13-001 aggregates). This module never
  invents accounts or metrics; a missing account stays BLOCKED and missing
  metrics stay explicitly uncertain.
