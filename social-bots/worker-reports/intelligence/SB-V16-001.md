## What was built

New `runtime/content_intelligence.py` — platform-native generation/repair that
preserves facts, novelty and experiment intent. Standalone; `pipeline.py`
unchanged.

### Model
- Content is ordered `Segment`s tagged `fact` or `framing`. A fact segment MUST
  carry an evidence binding (SB-V05-002 ref) and is never altered or dropped.
- `plan_variant(concept, platform)` renders a platform-native variant using
  `PLATFORM_CONSTRAINTS` (char limit, native format, alt-text, hashtag tail),
  with a **repair loop** that greedily keeps framing that fits and drops the
  rest — facts are always preserved verbatim.
- **Over-limit handling:** if facts alone exceed the limit the variant is
  WITHHELD with empty text (no silent truncation); otherwise it is repaired to
  READY. `facts_preserved()` asserts every fact's exact text is present.
- **Novelty/dedup:** exact content-id match plus word-shingle Jaccard
  near-duplicate detection (`similarity`, `check_novelty`).
- **Lineage:** series id, concept id, and `repurposed_from` recorded on every
  variant; `new_series()` / `repurpose()` manage series and repurpose lineage.
- One concept yields distinct platform-native variants (limits, format, hashtag
  tail differ per platform).
