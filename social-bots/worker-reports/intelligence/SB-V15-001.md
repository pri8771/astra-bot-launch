(engine persists under an `engine/` subnamespace).

### Model
- `Experiment`: hypothesis, baseline, intervention, primary metric, min
  observation hours, stop/safety criteria, status (draft/running/closed),
  outcome (SUCCESS/FAILURE/INCONCLUSIVE/STOPPED_SAFETY), result/effect size,
  learning refs.
- **Cannot close before the observation window** unless a safety stop fires
  (`can_close` / `close` enforce it).
- **Missing primary metric => INCONCLUSIVE, never SUCCESS.** A missing value
  (absent or `None`) is not treated as a zero effect.
- **Duplicate/overlap detection:** same bot+persona+primary metric+intervention
  while another is active is rejected on register.
- **Learning only through evidence refs:** `to_learning_ref()` yields a ref back
  to the experiment id/result only for a real (SUCCESS/FAILURE) effect;
  INCONCLUSIVE and STOPPED_SAFETY produce no ref (no fake learning).
