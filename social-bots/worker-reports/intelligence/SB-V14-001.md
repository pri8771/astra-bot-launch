## What was built

New `runtime/audience.py` — evidence-backed audience hypotheses that evolve only
with measured results.

### Model
- `Hypothesis`: id, segment/context, statement, raw supporting/contradicting
  `Observation` lists, created/last-updated, `forked_from`.
- **Observation vs inference separated:** observations are immutable stored facts
  carrying evidence refs (content/experiment/observation ids). Confidence is
  derived on read and never persisted.
- **No metrics => no fake learning:** an `Observation` must carry refs; a
  hypothesis with no (non-decayed) evidence is `unlearned` with confidence
  `None`. Confidence cannot move without real observations.
- **Bounded confidence:** neutral prior evidence ratio clamped to [0.05, 0.95].
  Repeated support raises confidence monotonically within the bound; contrary
  evidence lowers it.
- **Decay:** exponential half-life (default 30 days) so stale support fades; a
  lone year-old observation decays below the learning epsilon back to
  `unlearned`.
- **Contradiction handling:** `should_fork()` flags when contradicting evidence
  materially outweighs support; `fork_hypothesis()` spawns a rival carrying the
  contradicting evidence as its support, leaving the parent intact.
- **No sensitive-person profiling:** `validate_segment()` rejects segment keys or
  values naming protected/sensitive attributes.
