## Design (`runtime/reasoning.py` — `ContextualReasoningProvider`)
Every per-alternative estimate is COMPUTED from the context, not fixed:
- **evidence**: tag coverage, provenance (live-capture > operator-supplied >
  fixture), presence of a source → `strength`, `confidence`, `thin` flag;
- **persona**: `kind == cultural` / `cultural_review_required` and
  `evidence_required` add conservative bias and a create-penalty when unsourced;
- **state**: prior-hypotheses count lowers a new candidate's `novelty`
  (expected_learning);
- **duplication**: sets `duplication_risk` and boosts NO_ACTION.

Alternatives (NO_ACTION, RESEARCH_MORE, CREATE_CANDIDATE) are then ranked by the
policy's fixed utility weights (`Candidate.score`) — the estimates are the
reasoning, the weights are the policy's utility, so ranking varies with context.
`adaptive=False`: it is transparent deterministic reasoning, so under the
adaptive-required posture (SB-V04-001) it still fails closed; the model route
remains the adaptive one. Selected via `SBOTS_REASONING=contextual`.
