## What changed
- `decision.py`: a `policy` record block makes the boundary explicit —
  `provider_recommended` vs `policy_selected`, `recommended_followed`, a
  `selection_basis` ("deterministic policy ranking; recommended_action is advisory
  only") and the list of policy-owned controls (public_post, spend, messaging,
  review_gate, dedup, platform_limit, lease_fence, scheduling, effect_verification).
  Selection remains strictly by the policy's own ranking of validated alternatives.
- `decision.py`: an in-vocabulary-but-not-executable action (e.g.
  CONTINUE_EXPERIMENT) now yields a safe no-effect `blocked_unsupported_action`
  instead of an `unknown` result. Unsupported/unknown actions never reach the
  executor — `validate_proposal` (SB-V04-001) rejects them upstream.
- `worker.py`: finish receipt records `provider_recommended` + `policy_selected`,
  so the receipt clearly distinguishes proposal from allowed action.

## Policy ownership (unchanged, now asserted)

- leases/fencing (SB-V03-004), scheduling, effect verification — engine/worker.
