## What was built

- `WriterSpecialist` → `DraftResult/1`: claims are built only from `AnalysisResult/1` findings whose evidence resolves in the sandbox bundle; every claim carries its evidence refs; the `candidate` is shape-compatible with the pipeline draft observed in `runtime/pipeline.py::ideate` at this source (keys `content_id, bot, persona, signal_id, angle, hook, body, source_refs, signature_move, created_at, status`, recorded as `CANDIDATE_KEYS`); `status` is the constant `DRAFT_UNPUBLISHED`, re-imposed after any provider rewrite (a provider `status`/`candidate` payload is ignored and noted); provider claims without resolvable evidence are stripped and noted; no findings → empty candidate + `no supported claims`.
- `MediaBriefSpecialist` → `MediaBriefResult/1`: format, evidence-cited shots/frames, `sources` mirroring the evidence refs, `alt_text` `None` plus a limitation when missing (never an empty string); provider may supply alt text and evidence-cited shots only.
- Both reuse `RestrictedView`/`Bundle` from SB-S23-004 (one result file, inputs untouched), honour tool narrowing, refuse failed context, and time out via `Deadline`. No import of `pipeline`, `decision`, `content_intelligence`, `cultural_review`, `experiment_engine`, state or content stores; platform formatting and cultural review remain Intelligence gates at adoption/pipeline time.
