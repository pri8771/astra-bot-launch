## Problem

`emit()` previously did `find_session()` then append as separate steps. Concurrent
processes could both observe absence and both append, violating ONE SESSION =
ONE HEARTBEAT across processes.

## Change

- Wrap the find-then-append critical section in an exclusive `fcntl.flock` on
  `worker-reports/.session_heartbeat.emit.lock` (same POSIX pattern as
  `runtime/leasing.py`).
- Export `FLOCK_AVAILABLE` so hosts can refuse the weak non-POSIX path.
- Add `AtomicCrossProcessEmitTest`: 8 processes × 20 rounds racing the same
  `session_id`; assert exactly one durable winner and `n-1` duplicates each round.
